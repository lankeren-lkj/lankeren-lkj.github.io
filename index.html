Exhibition: MDW 2026 — Three Systems, Two Brands, One Practice

In 2026, Kejun Li will present three collections at Milano Design Week in collaboration with the Italian brands Bosa ceramiche and De Marchi Verona. The GEMINI collection for Bosa ceramiche will be showcased at Salone del Mobile (Rho Fiera), while CADENZA and FUGA, developed for De Marchi Verona, will be exhibited in the Brera Design District.

Across these works, Li continues to explore modular design as an open system—where geometry, material, and user interaction come together to generate evolving compositions in both objects and space.
